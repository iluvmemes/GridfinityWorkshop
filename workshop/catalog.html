Dimensions are engraved on ladder and spacing pieces. Working closure uses the actual clasp hinge, lid and buckle geometry, with lower body material removed.

Matched cartridge

Shared between cartridge and magazine. End hardware adds 12.2 mm to length. Flat base, without Gridfinity feet.

Interior

Centered array · 2 mm edge walls · adjustable edge-to-edge separation (minimum 1 mm). Clearance is added to each full dimension, not each side. Clasp end hardware is reserved in the capacity estimate.

Fixed 3 mm lid plate, with the dovetail below. Slides out through the front. Optional stacking lip sits on the lid. Print the plain lid top-face down; the stacking-lip version needs support under its plate overhangs when printed tongue-down.

Rounded bumps engage near closure. A relieved track keeps the rest of the slide clear. Start at 0.10 mm; printed retention depends on material and fit.

Four magnets: two in the lid tongue and two in rear interior ledges. Pockets are 2 mm deep. Check polarity before fitting; clearance pockets need adhesive. Independent of base magnets.

Pin fit

Allowance is added to the full diameter, not per side. Independent of buckle side play. Resulting bores must be 1.80–2.70 mm.

Clasp & grip

Extra play is total lateral clearance relative to the prototype: +0.20 mm means +0.10 mm per side. Pull-tab grip on both families; pin fit stays independent.

Indexing magazine

Seats use a 2 mm floor, rim-height end stops and 1.2 mm guide webs. Cutouts open both ends for buckle access; leave them off for a simple low cradle. Leave at least 10 mm of cartridge body above the guides. Unused seats stay solid.

Base retention magnets

Independent of stored magnets and their channel clearance.
